STOP 01

The turning

Two roads diverged in a wood, and I — I took the one less traveled by, and that has made all the difference.

Robert Frost

In early 2023, at 43, I was diagnosed with autism and ADHD. I had spent nearly two decades hand-building websites and web applications for corporate America, and that diagnosis turned out to be the hinge my life swung on. It made things harder than anything I had faced before. It also made them better than I knew how to want.

Code Less Traveled came out of that. It is neurodivergent-owned, it serves people from every walk of life, and it is one person: me. I connect people through technology — embracing the human element and wrangling the technical.

  • Start where you are.
  • Use what you have.
  • Do what you can.
  • Go where you are fully celebrated, not just tolerated.
